Privacy Policy

Game: Where in the World?  |  Effective Date: August 3, 2026

Overview

This privacy policy governs your use of the mobile application Where in the World? ("Application"), an interactive 3D globe geography and cartography game developed by VAR Games.

Data Collection & Privacy

We respect your privacy. The Application does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information. Specifically:

  • No account creation, sign-in, or registration is required.
  • We do not collect or track your device's real-world GPS location.
  • We do not track unique device identifiers, IP addresses, or personal identity.
  • We do not collect or harvest any usage analytics or telemetry data.

Local Device Storage

The Application stores basic gameplay settings locally on your device using local storage (Unity PlayerPrefs). This data includes:

  • Your local High Scores for each difficulty mode (Easy, Medium, Hard).
  • Your gameplay category filter preferences (Countries, Cities, Monuments).

This data remains 100% local on your physical device and is never uploaded to any cloud server or external service.

Children’s Privacy

The Application is family-friendly and safe for players of all ages, including children under the age of 13. We strictly adhere to COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) guidelines:

  • No personal data collection from any users.
  • No third-party advertisements or targeted ad tracking.
  • No in-app purchases or external link tracking.

Third-Party Services

The Application operates completely offline and self-contained. It does not integrate any third-party SDKs, ad networks, social media plugins, or external tracking services.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding privacy while playing Where in the World?, please feel free to reach out to us via our developer contact email listed on the official Google Play Store listing.
